# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, technically honest, and open to correction.
- Give actionable feedback and critique ideas rather than people.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and workplace obligations.
- Avoid sexualized language, personal attacks, intimidation, sustained disruption, or discriminatory conduct.
- Do not publish private information or proprietary manufacturing data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project. Enforcement decisions are made by the project maintainer in good faith and may consider context, severity, and prior conduct.
